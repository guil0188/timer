Finger Training Method 1: Repeater

Benefits of "Repeater":

This method of training builds muscular hypertrophy and local endurance in the forearm as well as finger endurance/capacity

  • Execution: Hang for 7 seconds, rest for 3 seconds, Repeat 6 times = 1 set
  • Rest: Rest 3 minutes between sets
  • Volume: 2-4 sets per session
  • Frequency: 1-2 times per week if your a frequent climber (climbs 2-3 times per week or more), 2 if your a infrequent climper (climbs once a week)
  • Never train 3+ times a week: Training repeaters too frequently dramtoically increases your risk of pulley strains and tendinitis
  • Intensity: Use 70% - 80% of your maximum hanging weight when hanging for 7 seconds
